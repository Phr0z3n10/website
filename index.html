Responsible AI · Human-Centered Language Technology

I study how AI systems understand and evaluate real-world language, with a focus on speech, multilinguality, dialect variation, and equitable model evaluation.

What I’m working on now

  • Speech

    Dialect-aware speech evaluation

    Identifying when ASR systems distort or normalize real linguistic variation, and telling that apart from genuine recognition failure.

  • Multilingual

    Multilingual and code-switched speech

    How switching between languages mid-utterance affects recognition performance, and why standard metrics misread it.

  • Practice

    Responsible AI in practice

    Connecting fairness and human-centered design research to how AI is actually governed, evaluated, and deployed.
